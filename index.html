06 / 06 — FAQ

You've got questions.
We've got answers.

How do you price engagements?

+

It depends on scope. Strategy roadmaps, fractional leadership retainers, and engineering projects each have different structures. We'll give you an honest quote after a discovery call. No surprises, no hidden fees.

How fast can you deliver?

+

We work in focused sprints. Strategy roadmaps in 2–4 weeks. Engineering projects from 4–12 weeks depending on complexity. We move fast because your competitive window is shrinking.

Do you work with startups or only enterprises?

+

Both. We've worked with Series B fintechs and Fortune 500 retailers. What matters is that you're serious about building production AI — not just running experiments.

What makes you different from big consulting firms?

+

No layers. No overhead. You work directly with senior practitioners who've actually built AI systems — not junior analysts reading playbooks. We ship, not just advise.

Why wouldn't this be a fit?

+

If you're looking for a vendor to hand off to without any collaboration, or if you want AI for a press release rather than production — we're probably not the right partner.
